# ASSET LICENSES & PROVENANCE — TOMORROW'S YOU

All assets used in **TOMORROW'S YOU** are custom-created and procedurally generated for this project.

## Visual Assets
- **Player & Echo Avatars**: Custom vector/procedural geometry rendered in Godot 2D with neon sci-fi shader/canvas styling. Created specifically for this project.
- **Tiles (Walls, Floors, Plates, Doors, Portals)**: Original vector and procedural textures created in Godot.
- **Icons & UI**: Original SVG family in `assets/ui/icons/` (24 × 24 viewbox, 1.8-unit main stroke, round caps and joins), plus procedural Godot controls. White vectors inherit semantic button tints. `assets/icons/ui/` remains a legacy compatibility fallback. The split-orbit Create Echo emblem represents the operator and a displaced temporal teammate.
- **Bundled fonts**: Oxanium (headings), Inter (interface/body), and IBM Plex Mono (technical metadata), distributed under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are bundled in `assets/fonts/OFL_LICENSES/`.

## Audio Assets
- **Sound Effects (SFX)**: Procedurally synthesized audio waveforms (chirps, clicks, hums, sweeps, power-ups, victory chords) generated at runtime via Godot AudioStreamWAV generation.
- **Music**: Ambient synth pads and harmonic textures synthesized in-engine via procedural audio generators.
